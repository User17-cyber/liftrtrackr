    About

    Nutrients from USDA FoodData Central; foods identified by Claude. Energy from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and, once you have the data, from your own meals and weigh-ins. Estimates for tracking, not medical advice.

    Sources
    1. USDA Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central: SR Legacy (April 2018) and FNDDS 2021–2023 (October 2024).
    2. Mifflin MD, St Jeor ST, et al. Am J Clin Nutr 1990;51:241–7. Accuracy review: Frankenfield D, et al. J Am Diet Assoc 2005;105:775–89.
    3. Roza AM, Shizgal HM (revised Harris-Benedict). Am J Clin Nutr 1984;40:168–82. Katch-McArdle: 370 + 21.6 × lean mass (kg).
    4. Institute of Medicine, Dietary Reference Intakes for Energy, Carbohydrate, Fiber, Fat, Fatty Acids, Cholesterol, Protein, and Amino Acids, 2005.
    5. Weight trend: exponentially smoothed average, 10% per day, after Walker J, The Hacker's Diet (1991). Energy balance uses about 7,700 kcal per kg (Hall KD, Int J Obes 2008;32:573–6).
    6. Estimated one-rep max: Epley B, Poundage Chart, Boyd Epley Workout, 1985: weight × (1 + reps / 30).
